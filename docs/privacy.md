# ReelPilot AI Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 9, 2026**

ReelPilot AI helps creators understand audience comments and turn recurring questions into content ideas and editable Reel content packs. This policy explains what information ReelPilot AI collects, how it is used, and how you can request deletion.

## Information we collect

When you use ReelPilot AI, we may collect:

- Account information such as your email address, display name, password hash, and workspace information.
- Comments that you upload as a CSV or import from a connected Instagram professional account.
- Instagram account information made available through Meta's authorization flow, such as your Instagram user ID and username.
- Content generated from your imported comments, including audience signals, topic clusters, scripts, captions, hooks, calls to action, and hashtags.
- Basic technical and usage information needed to authenticate users, enforce workspace limits, protect the service, and keep the application reliable.

ReelPilot AI does not ask for or store your Instagram password. When you connect Instagram, we store the access credential required to make authorized API requests for your workspace. Keep your account credentials private and disconnect the integration when you no longer want ReelPilot AI to access Instagram data.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Import and organize audience comments.
- Identify repeated questions and audience demand.
- Generate editable content ideas and draft scripts.
- Authenticate your account and isolate your workspace data.
- Track usage limits, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve the product.

ReelPilot AI does not publish Reels or comments to Instagram unless a future feature explicitly asks for that permission and you authorize it. Content packs are drafts for your review.

## AI processing

Depending on the configuration, analysis and content generation may run locally through Ollama or through a configured third-party AI provider such as OpenAI. When a third-party provider is enabled, relevant comment text and generation instructions may be sent to that provider to perform the requested operation. Do not upload sensitive personal information that you do not want processed by the selected provider.

## Instagram and Meta

If you connect Instagram, ReelPilot AI uses Meta's Instagram APIs and receives only the permissions you approve. Meta may process information under its own privacy policy and terms. You can remove ReelPilot AI's access from Instagram or Accounts Center's connected apps settings. You can also use the Disconnect Instagram action inside ReelPilot AI, which removes the stored Instagram credential from the ReelPilot AI workspace.

## Sharing and storage

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate authentication, hosting, databases, error monitoring, or AI features. Access is limited to what is needed for those services.

During local development, ReelPilot AI stores application data in a local SQLite database. A deployed instance may use a managed database selected by the operator. Storage location and retention can therefore depend on the deployment configuration.

## Retention

We keep information while your account or workspace is active, or as needed to provide the service and meet legal or security obligations. Imported comments, analyses, and content packs remain in the workspace until they are deleted by the workspace operator or the service is reset according to its deployment configuration.

## Your choices and deletion requests

You can:

- Disconnect Instagram from ReelPilot AI.
- Delete imported comments, analyses, and generated content through the workspace operator or deployment administrator.
- Request deletion of your account and associated workspace data.
- Ask what personal information is stored about you.

To request deletion or ask a privacy question, email **kavyansh.pandey27@gmail.com** with the subject `ReelPilot AI data deletion request`. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before completing a request. We aim to respond within 30 days.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for the information handled by the application. No online service or local development environment can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly using the privacy contact above.

## Children's privacy

ReelPilot AI is intended for creators and businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ReelPilot AI changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the current version was published.
